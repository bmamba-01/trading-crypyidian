# WP06 first-party QA fixture

## Identity and scope

`CRYPIDIAN_FIRST_PARTY_QA` names the repository-owned synthetic fixture used to exercise WP06 replay, provenance, scorer TEST access, and current-rights checks in isolated Compose E2E databases. Its market bars use the separate `CRYPIDIAN_FIRST_PARTY_QA_FIXTURE` source label and `crypidian-first-party-qa:v1:` cursor prefix.

The fixture is generated by `tests/fixtures/runtime_replay.py::build_h03_source_pinned_dataset_replay`. The bars and trade outcomes are deterministic software test data. They are not Binance data, licensed exchange history, a market sample, strategy performance evidence, or a readiness assessment.

## Rights policy

The E2E journey persists a `CRYPIDIAN_FIRST_PARTY_QA` Source Registry entry and reviewed policy in its newly created workspace, then obtains a short current-rights lease through `SourceRegistryBackfillRightsGate`. The policy evidence URL is this repository document. The normal manifest, bundle, replay, market-bar persistence, outcome, opportunity, candidate proof, rights receipt, and one-shot scorer TEST paths consume that policy.

The application and database accept this exact provider and evidence document only in the QA source path. Binance REST request and ingestion validation remain restricted to `BINANCE_USDM_REST` and `BINANCE_USDM_REST_BACKFILL`.

## Deployment boundary

First-party QA evidence is never eligible to activate `SHADOW` or `PAPER_GATE`. The database deployment-rights gateway rejects a QA bundle before it can return deployment eligibility. Compose runs use a unique project name, workspace UUID, PostgreSQL service, and database volume lifecycle per test, so generated QA reports remain inside the disposable E2E database. Tests must assert that rejected activation leaves the deployment pointer unchanged.

## Reproducibility

The scorer fixture uses H03-generated outcomes with nine balanced `loss`, `flat`, and `profit` labels in its held-out TEST fold. The approved v1 policy requires at least 1,000 samples and at least 100 samples per class, so this report must remain `INCONCLUSIVE`. The fixture exercises persisted rights, proof, one-shot report, and fail-closed deployment denial only; it provides no evidence of live or historical trading performance.
